# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and curious. Disagreement is welcome; harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, and publishing another person's private information are not.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate this standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This is a compact project policy. As the contributor community grows, it will be replaced by a full community-reviewed code of conduct.
